About Me

I am an AI Researcher in ADD. I received my M.S. in Artificial Intelligence from the KAIST Graduate School of AI, where I was advised by Prof. Kijung Shin. I earned my B.S. in Computer Science and Engineering from POSTECH. My research spans a broad range of AI applications, including data mining and computer vision for applied systems. At the Agency for Defense Development (ADD), I spent three years researching object detection for aerial platforms. Currently, I am working on open-world object detection (OWOD) for edge devices.
